# StashPost

Mail the items you choose to your alts, each to the right one, when you open a mailbox.

For **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Source code, issues and releases: [github.com/yannlugrin/StashPost](https://github.com/yannlugrin/StashPost).

> **In development.** Nothing is built yet: this page describes what StashPost will do.

## Features

- *Coming:* **Rules by category.** Choose what goes where by the game's item categories (Trade Goods › Herb to your alchemist, Metal & Stone to your blacksmith), by quality (every Uncommon item to your enchanter), or item by item. A specific item comes first, then a subcategory, then a category, then quality.
- *Coming:* **Keep some in your bags.** For a specific item, keep a number in your bags and send only the rest, or don't send it.
- *Coming:* **Shared by all your characters.** The rules are the same for every character; a character can override a rule for itself: another recipient, don't send, another number to keep, other qualities.
- *Coming:* **Any alt.** Pick from the characters you logged in with StashPost, or type the name of an alt on another account.
- *Coming:* **At the mailbox.** A panel next to the mailbox lists what will be sent, alt by alt, with the postage; uncheck an item to keep it this time. Then it sends mail after mail and shows each one sent, or failed with the game's reason.
- *Coming:* **Automatic or on demand.** Send as soon as the mailbox opens, or only when you click Send. A key you choose (Shift by default) does the opposite while you hold it, like the game's auto-loot key.

Open the rules window with the minimap button, the add-on compartment next to the minimap, or `/stashpost open`. Right-click the button for a menu.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/stashpost` | List the commands |
| `/stashpost open` | Open the rules window |
| `/stashpost settings` | Open the StashPost settings |

## Looks

The windows use the default game look. With EllesmereUI installed, they take your EllesmereUI theme.
